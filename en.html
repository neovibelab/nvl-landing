Method

Start in music, follow the vibe anywhere

Music went through the digital transition first. From file sharing to streaming subscriptions, from fan clubs to fandom commerce. So the future of other industries shows up here early. K-pop, fandom, IP, live and retail all sit inside music.

A vibe can start anywhere. A hairline crack in a cult scene, a subculture's code, the cultural grain of a single city in an emerging market. AI is strong on formalized signals like charts and virality, but the place where a cultural vibe begins still has to be read with human sense and accumulated context. That is why we watch five regions every day, Korea, global, China, Japan and Southeast Asia, and go deep by region.

The transfer runs both ways. We carry principles proven first in entertainment into other industries, and we read what games, brands and spaces take away when they enter music. Every piece is written on a grid of production, distribution and consumption crossed with seven factors. The grid and the seventeen frames are in the library .
